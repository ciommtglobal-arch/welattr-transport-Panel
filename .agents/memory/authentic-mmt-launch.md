name: Authentic MMT Global launch
description: Authenticity and audience-growth boundary for the MMT Global public launch.
---

MMT Global’s public contact identity must use a real dedicated business phone number and real official social accounts. Do not publish guessed, temporary, placeholder, or personal contact details.

No public sharing or audience-growth activity should begin before the business line, WhatsApp Business, and official social profiles are complete. Once the setup is finished, launch with an organized campaign and consider paid advertising when it improves targeted reach.

The initial launch content should remain fully controlled by MMT Global: real branded visuals and approved posts are prepared as drafts, reviewed, and published selectively. Member-submitted content should enter a review queue before public visibility during this phase.

**Why:** The website represents a real holding and business network; trust, verification, consistent ownership, and a coordinated first impression matter more than early visibility.

**How to apply:** Finish the dedicated +90 business line, WhatsApp Business, and official social profiles first. Then prepare branded launch content, review every draft, connect the exact verified URLs to the website, and begin a joint organic audience-growth plan. Do not use purchased or fake followers.

Customer communication starts on WhatsApp Business until revenue justifies a staffed office operation. Early outbound content, campaign decisions, and important customer responses should pass through the owner’s approval. Reinvest initial earnings into a staffed customer-service desk, shared operational equipment, stronger brand identity, and controlled infrastructure growth.

**Why:** Starting with the real business line keeps costs low while preserving a direct customer channel; premature office and team costs can consume capital before demand is proven.

**How to apply:** Use WhatsApp Business labels, approved quick replies, a simple customer log, and escalation rules first. Add a shared inbox/CRM and role-based team access only when volume and revenue make them useful. The assistant does not monitor WhatsApp directly unless an official integration is later connected.
