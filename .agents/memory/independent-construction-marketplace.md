---
name: Independent construction marketplace
description: Branding and representation limits for marketing Piyadan products through MMT Global.
---

MMT Global has not formally obtained Piyadan regional distributorship. The construction store must use an independent marketplace brand and must not claim to be Piyadan's authorized or exclusive distributor.

**Why:** The commercial goal is to market genuine Piyadan products and build sales before any formal distributorship exists; inaccurate affiliation claims would create legal and trust risks.

**How to apply:** Present Piyadan only as the manufacturer/brand of genuine listed products when product information and media are authorized. Label MMT Global as the independent seller or marketplace operator, and reserve distributor language until written authorization is provided.
