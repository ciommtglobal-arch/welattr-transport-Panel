---
name: Public logistics hub
description: Durable separation between logistics publishing and the physical-products storefront.
---

MMT Global's public logistics surface combines approved logistics B2B listings with published route, transport, and advertising media. Shopify remains a separate physical-products system and should not become the logistics content hub.

**Why:** Logistics discovery depends on routes, capacity, service providers, and announcements rather than product inventory. Mixing those concerns into Shopify would create misleading catalog semantics and weaken the free B2B network.

**How to apply:** Use public-safe listing APIs and published-media filters for the logistics hub; keep private files behind short-lived signed redirects and preserve Shopify for real construction-product catalog, inventory, and checkout flows.
