---
name: MMT Connect verification
description: The product direction for international B2B business onboarding and self-publishing.
---

MMT Connect is the international-facing name for MMT Global's free B2B network. A business submits real legal/registration information and an official tax or equivalent document once. After MMT verifies the business profile, the owner may publish their own listings without per-listing approval. Public listings may include the firm's website and media links, while verification documents remain private.

**Why:** The user wants fast network growth among tradespeople, factories, producers, buyers, logistics providers, and service businesses without making MMT a manual approval bottleneck. One-time identity/business verification preserves trust while allowing the network and its media reach to grow.

**How to apply:** Keep browsing and core participation free. Require a signed-in business account for verification and publishing. Use staff review only for business verification, plus suspension/reporting controls for later abuse. Keep shopping/transactions and follower features separate from the initial verification milestone.

The rollout priority is media and audience growth first; commerce, checkout, and transaction features come after the network and MMT Global media channels have established reach.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the near-term effort concentrated on media before converting the network into a commercial channel.

**How to apply:** Prioritize content distribution, social/media links, audience acquisition, discoverability, and traffic back to MMT Global properties before building marketplace payments or shopping flows.

All MMT Connect business-account decisions must pass through MMT staff: verification acceptance/rejection, suspension, listing review where applicable, and user complaints.

**Why:** The user wants MMT to retain trust and quality control while allowing verified businesses to publish without waiting for approval on every routine listing.

**How to apply:** Keep staff-only queues protected by Clerk plus the active staff table. Store reviewer identity, decision status, timestamps, and internal notes. Never expose verification documents or internal review data publicly.

MMT Global should feel corporate and modern without becoming exclusive: anyone should be able to discover the network, while businesses, buyers, workers, and the public can benefit from free core access.

**Why:** The user wants a trusted international trade network that serves both established business people and people with limited resources, with broad public satisfaction rather than a closed premium club.

**How to apply:** Keep public browsing open, make basic participation free, use clear trust and satisfaction signals, support mobile/low-bandwidth access and plain language, and design separate paths for businesses, buyers, and the public.

The network must support both supply and demand: verified businesses can publish offers, while signed-in individuals or organizations can submit buyer requests that MMT reviews before public publication.

**Why:** A trade network cannot depend only on sellers; visible buyer demand gives producers and service providers a concrete reason to participate and lets the public benefit from the network.

**How to apply:** Keep approved buyer requests publicly searchable, protect request submission with account identity, and give MMT staff a dedicated review queue with approve, reject, and close decisions.

MMT Connect employment uses two paths: signed-in people submit moderated “İş arıyorum” requests, while verified businesses publish “İşveren / İş ilanı” listings with employment details.

**Why:** Employment demand and employer supply need different trust controls: individuals need MMT review before publication, while a verified business can publish its own vacancy through the existing business-account model.

**How to apply:** Keep employment requests under the staff request queue; keep employer listings under verified-business publishing and the public listing filters. Preserve employment type, salary, and experience fields through API, moderation, and public detail views.

MMT Connect should become MMT Global's own branded trade network; Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Telegram are distribution channels rather than the core product.

**Why:** The user wants to protect and grow the MMT brand through an owned network instead of depending permanently on external social platforms.

**How to apply:** Use external channels to attract attention and traffic, but keep business profiles, buyer demand, listings, trust decisions, conversations, and network relationships on MMT-owned infrastructure.
