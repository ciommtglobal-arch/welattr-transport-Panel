---
name: MMT Connect verification
description: The product direction for international B2B business onboarding and self-publishing.
---

MMT Connect is the international-facing name for MMT Global's free B2B network. A business submits real legal/registration information and an official tax or equivalent document once. After MMT verifies the business profile, the owner may publish their own listings without per-listing approval. Public listings may include the firm's website and media links, while verification documents remain private.

**Why:** The user wants fast network growth among tradespeople, factories, producers, buyers, logistics providers, and service businesses without making MMT a manual approval bottleneck. One-time identity/business verification preserves trust while allowing the network and its media reach to grow.

**How to apply:** Keep browsing and core participation free. Require a signed-in business account for verification and publishing. Use staff review only for business verification, plus suspension/reporting controls for later abuse. Keep shopping/transactions and follower features separate from the initial verification milestone.

The rollout priority is media and audience growth first; commerce, checkout, and transaction features come after the network and MMT Global media channels have established reach.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the near-term effort concentrated on media before converting the network into a commercial channel.

**How to apply:** Prioritize content distribution, social/media links, audience acquisition, discoverability, and traffic back to MMT Global properties before building marketplace payments or shopping flows.

All MMT Connect business-account decisions must pass through MMT staff: verification acceptance/rejection, suspension, listing review where applicable, and user complaints.

**Why:** The user wants MMT to retain trust and quality control while allowing verified businesses to publish without waiting for approval on every routine listing.

**How to apply:** Keep staff-only queues protected by Clerk plus the active staff table. Store reviewer identity, decision status, timestamps, and internal notes. Never expose verification documents or internal review data publicly.
