---
name: MMT Connect delegation boundary
description: Owner's stated approval boundary when delegating company operations to an area leader.
---

An area leader may coordinate and manage internal company requests and tasks, but the company owner retains the final affirmative approval. Do not describe area-leader access as authority to approve on the owner's behalf. Even low-risk work and owner-submitted work must not be treated as finally approved without an explicit owner decision.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose broad operational coordination for an area leader only on the condition that the final approval remains with them. Automatic approval would bypass that boundary.

**How to apply:** In future invitation, workflow, or permissions changes, keep owner-only affirmative approval and explain any separate preliminary review or rejection rights before granting them.
