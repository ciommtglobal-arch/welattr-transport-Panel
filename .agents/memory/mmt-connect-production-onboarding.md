---
name: MMT Connect production onboarding
description: Production business access must be established by the founder account instead of copying development records.
---

Replit-managed Clerk users and the app's development and production databases are isolated. A verified development owner and company profile do not establish production ownership or membership.

**Why:** The published app can be healthy while its production company tables are empty, so copying or recreating records for employees would risk duplicate ownership and incorrect authority.

**How to apply:** Have the real founder sign in to the published app and submit the single company verification profile there. After that owner record exists, use the production panel to create employee invitations; employees should only accept links addressed to their own Clerk account email.
